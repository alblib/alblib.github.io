## Introduction
## Default form
$$
\left|x\right|^n + \left|y\right|^n = 1
$$
$$
x=\mathrm{sgn}\left(\cos\theta\right)\cdot\left|\cos \theta\right|^\frac{2}{n}
\\
y=\mathrm{sgn}\left(\sin\theta\right)\cdot\left|\sin \theta\right|^\frac{2}{n}
$$
### Comparison with Round Rectangle
### Concentric form
$$
\left(\frac{dx}{dt}, \frac{dy}{dt}\right)
=\frac{2}{n}\left(
-\sin\theta\left|\cos\theta\right|^{\frac{2}{n}-1}
,
\cos\theta\left|\sin\theta\right|^{\frac{2}{n}-1}
\right)
$$

normal
$$
\sim\left(\frac{dy}{dt}, -\frac{dx}{dt}\right)
\sim\left(
\cos\theta\left|\sin\theta\right|^{\frac{2}{n}-1}
,
\sin\theta\left|\cos\theta\right|^{\frac{2}{n}-1}
\right)
$$
## Rectangle form
$$
\left|\frac{x}{a}\right|^m + \left|\frac{y}{b}\right|^n = 1
